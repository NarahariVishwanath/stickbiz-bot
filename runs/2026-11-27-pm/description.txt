📰 Today's stories (27 Nov 2026):
📉 Sensex closed 989 points lower and Nifty ended near 25,600 as traders booked profits and IT shares fell.

💱 The rupee slipped 24 paise to 92.62 per dollar in early trade as oil rose above $95 a barrel.
