HOOK: The Sensex lost 989 points today. And a ₹28,000 crore Tata deal is under Sebi's watch.

▶ Sensex ends 989 points down (3 sources)
The Sensex closed 989 points lower. The Nifty ended near 25,600. Traders booked profits after a two-day rally. Information technology shares fell the most. Rising oil prices, a weaker rupee and doubts over the India-US trade deal also weighed.

▶ Sebi studies Tata Steel deal (2 sources)
Sebi is studying the Tata Steel and APL Apollo deal. It is weighing whether to judge all parts of the deal together. APL Apollo would buy Tata Steel's tubes businesses for ₹28,000 crore, funded by borrowing. Minority shareholders are worried about that debt. APL Apollo shares rebounded about 4% in early trade after a sharp fall.

▶ Tata Sons fight hits court (3 sources)
The Tata Sons power struggle has reached court. Two trusts that control Tata Sons asked the Mumbai High Court to declare the reappointment of chairman N. Chandrasekaran invalid. Tata Trusts holds 66% of Tata Sons. The Tata Sons board backs a possible stock market listing, but the charity arm does not.

▶ Rupee slips as oil climbs (2 sources)
The rupee slipped 24 paise to 92.62 per dollar in early trade. Oil prices rose above $95 a barrel after vessels were reportedly targeted near the Strait of Hormuz. Steady foreign fund inflows and a softer dollar limited the fall.

▶ Mahanadi lists below IPO price (3 sources)
Mahanadi Coalfields, a Coal India unit, listed at a 2% discount to its ₹136 issue price. The ₹8,981 crore IPO was subscribed only 1.52 times. All the shares on sale came from Coal India, the parent.
