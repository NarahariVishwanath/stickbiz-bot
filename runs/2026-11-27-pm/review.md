Sebi is studying the Tata Steel and APL Apollo deal. It is weighing whether to judge all parts of the deal together. **TATA SONS — Tata Sons fight hits court** · _3 sources: Business Standard, Economic Times, Hindu BusinessLine_

The Tata Sons power struggle has reached court. Two trusts that control Tata Sons asked the Mumbai High Court to declare the reappointment of chairman N. Chandrasekaran invalid. Tata Trusts holds 66% of Tata Sons. The Tata Sons board backs a possible stock market listing, but the charity arm does not.

- 🖼️ *clip* · **TATA VS / TATA?** — The group's **controlling trusts** and its **board** disagree, so the fight went to court.

**RUPEE — Rupee slips as oil climbs** · _2 sources: Business Standard, Hindu BusinessLine_

The rupee slipped 24 paise to 92.62 per dollar in early trade. Oil prices rose above $95 a barrel after vessels were reportedly targeted near the Strait of Hormuz. Steady foreign fund inflows and a softer dollar limited the fall.

- 🖼️ *bignum* · **RUPEE / SLIPS 24 PAISE** — The currency weakened to **₹92.62 per dollar** as oil prices recovered.
- 🖼️ *icons* · **OIL ABOVE / $95 A BARREL** — Reports of **vessels being targeted** near the Strait of Hormuz raised **supply worries.**
- 🖼️ *icons* · **WHAT / LIMITED IT** — **Foreign fund inflows** and a **softer dollar** kept the fall small.

Mahanadi Coalfields, a Coal India unit, listed at a 2% discount to its ₹136 issue price. The ₹8,981 crore IPO was subscribed only 1.52 times. All the shares on sale came from Coal India, the parent.
