**MARKET UPDATE — Mixed start, weaker rupee** · _2 sources: Business Standard, Hindu BusinessLine_

Sensex and Nifty were mixed in morning trade on Thursday. The Sensex was up 0.17% at 84,076.82, while the Nifty was down 0.10% at 25,793.20. The rupee slipped 20 paise to 92.40 against the dollar. Oil above $95 a barrel and the US tariff plan raised dollar demand, traders said. Still, the rupee has recovered nearly 5% from its record low close of 96.88 on 8 October.

On screen: “Indices split in morning / Sensex up, Nifty down” → “Rupee loses 20 paise / Now at 92.40 per dollar” → “Oil and tariffs hit rupee / Still up 5% from low”

**US TARIFF — 17% or 25%: deal decides** · _2 sources: Business Standard, Hindu BusinessLine_

The US has proposed new tariffs on 14 trading partners, India included. India would face 17% if a trade deal is signed, and up to 25% without one. With a deal, Bangladesh would face 18%, and Vietnam and China 20%. Think tank GTRI says the two-rate design is a way to push countries into deals. India rejects the claim of excess capacity and says it wants an early trade deal.

On screen: “New US tariff plan / 14 partners, India included” → “India: 17% with a deal / Up to 25% without one” → “Rivals face higher rates / Vietnam, China: 20%” → “GTRI questions the plan / India wants an early deal”

**TATA SONS — Tata Sons board showdown** · _2 sources: Business Standard, Mint_

The Tata Sons board will meet on 30 November, after the date moved from 26 November. The RBI told Tata Sons on 16 November to follow listing rules for large finance companies. Tata Trusts chairman Noel Tata wants his own issues discussed first, and prefers a plan to keep Tata Sons private. Tata Trusts is also taking the chairman's reappointment to the Bombay High Court.

On screen: “Tata Sons board meets / Now on 30 November” → “RBI says: follow listing rules / Letter dated 16 November” → “Noel Tata wants his items / Discussed first”

**BOND MARKET — Adani plans public bonds** · _2 sources: Business Standard, Hindu BusinessLine_

Adani Enterprises and Adani Power plan public bond sales to raise at least ₹2,000 crore, according to people familiar. One offer is aimed for the end of December and the other for March. The terms are not final. Indian firms have raised ₹4,840 crore through public bonds this fiscal year, against ₹11,340 crore in all of the last one.

On screen: “Adani firms eye bonds / At least ₹2,000 crore” → “Public bonds still thin / ₹4,840 crore this year”

**COMMODITIES — Gold and silver bounce** · _2 sources: Economic Times, Mint_

Gold rose about 1% and silver about 3% on the MCX on Thursday. Traders still expect the US Federal Reserve to raise interest rates.

On screen: “Metals bounce on MCX / Silver leads, up about 3%”
