📰 Today's stories (26 Nov 2026):
📊 Sensex edged up 0.17% to 84,076.82 and Nifty dipped 0.10% to 25,793.20 in morning trade; the rupee slipped 20 paise to 92.40.
🌐 The US proposed a 17% tariff on India if a trade deal is signed and up to 25% without one; Bangladesh would face 18% and Vietnam and China 20% with a deal.
🏢 Tata Sons' board meets on 30 November; the RBI has repeated that listing rules apply, and Noel Tata wants his issues taken up first.
💰 Adani Enterprises and Adani Power plan public bonds to raise at least ₹2,000 crore; Indian firms have raised ₹4,840 crore this fiscal year via public bonds.
🥇 MCX gold gained about 1% and silver about 3% on Thursday while rate-hike expectations in the US remain.
