HOOK: A ₹28,000 crore deal has SEBI watching closely. And the RBI changes a key rule for banks.

▶ SEBI eyes Tata-APL deal (3 sources)
SEBI is looking closely at the Tata Steel and APL Apollo deal. Tata Steel will sell its tubes businesses to APL Apollo for ₹28,000 crore. Proxy advisers warn that minority shareholders could carry the cost. SEBI has now given Tata Steel until 8 December to publish its open offer statement.

▶ RBI eases daily CRR rule (3 sources)
From 1 December, the RBI will ease a rule for banks. The daily minimum for the cash reserve ratio falls from 99% to 90%. Banking liquidity has slipped into deficit, pushing short-term rates higher. A 25 basis point rate hike is widely expected at the next policy review.

▶ Zee buyback opens 1 Dec (3 sources)
Zee Entertainment's ₹600 crore share buyback opens on 1 December and closes on 7 December. The price is ₹91 per share. The promoters will not take part.

▶ Huang returns to Nvidia (3 sources)
Jensen Huang is back as Nvidia's CEO, after resigning on 12 November. Nvidia shares jumped sharply on the news. Donald Trump says he helped bring him back. US stocks rose on Friday, with tech leading.
