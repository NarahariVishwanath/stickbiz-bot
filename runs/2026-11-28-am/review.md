**RBI CRR — RBI eases daily CRR rule** · _3 sources: Business Standard, Hindu BusinessLine, Mint_

From 1 December, the RBI will ease a rule for banks. The daily minimum for the cash reserve ratio falls from 99% to 90%. Banking liquidity has slipped into deficit, pushing short-term rates higher. A 25 basis point rate hike is widely expected at the next policy review.

- 🖼️ *clip* · **RBI EASES / BANK RULE** — CRR is the slice of deposits that banks must keep with the **RBI**, and it earns **no interest.**
- 🖼️ *compare* · **99% TO / 90%** — The overall CRR stays at **3%**; only the **daily** minimum is relaxed.
- 🖼️ *icons* · **CASH IS / GETTING TIGHT** — With the system in **deficit**, short-term borrowing costs have **hardened.**
- 🖼️ *bignum* · **RATE HIKE / AHEAD?** — Economists widely expect a **25 basis point hike**, though some see room for a **larger one.**
