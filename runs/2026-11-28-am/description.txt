📰 Today's stories (28 Nov 2026):
🏛️ SEBI is examining the ₹28,000 crore Tata Steel–APL Apollo deal and gave Tata Steel until 8 December for its open offer statement.
🏦 From 1 December the RBI cuts the daily CRR minimum from 99% to 90% as banking liquidity turns to deficit; a 25 bps hike is widely expected.
💰 Zee Entertainment's ₹600 crore buyback at ₹91 per share runs from 1 to 7 December; promoters are not participating.
🇺🇸 Jensen Huang returned as Nvidia CEO after resigning on 12 November; Nvidia jumped and US stocks rose on Friday.
