Wall Street fell sharply on Wednesday as AI stocks were sold. The Nasdaq 100 briefly dropped 20% from its record close. US core inflation hit 3.3%, above the 3.2% expected.

Tata Steel may rework its deal with APL Apollo Tubes. APL Apollo would pay about ₹28,000 crore, mostly debt, for Tata Steel's tubes businesses. Investors are worried, and talks are on with no conclusion yet.

▶ Simpler KYC from 24 Dec (2 sources)
SEBI is easing KYC rules from 24 December. Common extra KYC details will be stored centrally at KYC registration agencies. Investors will not need to submit them again to every intermediary.

▶ Airtel's Assam data centres (2 sources)
Airtel will invest about ₹1,200 crore in three data centres in Assam. They are planned in Guwahati, Jorhat and Silchar, and could be ready by 2028.
