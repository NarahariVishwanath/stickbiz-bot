HOOK: A ₹28,000 crore Tata deal is under fire. And the Tata family fight has reached court.

▶ Tata Steel deal draws questions (2 sources)
Proxy advisers want SEBI to look closely at the ₹28,000 crore Tata Steel and APL Apollo deal. APL Apollo would buy two Tata Steel businesses, paid for with debt. Tata Steel would also buy the promoters' stake for ₹15,510 crore. APL Apollo shares have fallen more than 30% since the deal was announced.

▶ Tata Trusts move High Court (2 sources)
Tata Trusts have gone to the Bombay High Court. They challenge the reappointment of N Chandrasekaran as Tata Sons chairman. The court hears the case on Friday, before the Tata Sons board meeting on 30 November. The trusts also seek damages of ₹50 crore and ₹15 crore.

▶ Markets rise, cautious start (2 sources)
Indian markets rose for a second day on Thursday. The Sensex gained 390 points and the Nifty gained 65 points. Analysts expect a cautious start today, after a strong rally.

▶ NSE gets RBI nod for bond futures (2 sources)
The RBI has cleared the National Stock Exchange to bring in a new product. These are futures that track corporate bond indices.

▶ Nvidia pauses Asia shipments (3 sources)
Nvidia has asked partners to pause server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia. Dell and Supermicro told buyers their orders will not be sent. This follows new US export curbs on advanced AI chips. It is unclear if the pause is temporary.
